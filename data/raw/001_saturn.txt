Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in the Solar System, after Jupiter. It is a gas giant with an average radius about nine times that of Earth. Saturn is best known for its prominent ring system, made up mostly of ice particles, with a smaller amount of rocky debris and dust. Saturn has 146 confirmed moons, the largest of which is Titan, the second-largest moon in the Solar System. Titan is the only moon known to have a dense atmosphere and stable liquid on its surface, in the form of lakes and rivers of methane and ethane. A year on Saturn lasts about 29.5 Earth years, while a day on Saturn lasts only about 10.7 hours.
